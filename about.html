About Me

I started in data analytics — building dashboards, writing SQL queries, and turning spreadsheets into stories. But the more I worked with data, the more I wanted to go deeper: not just reporting on what happened, but building the systems that make the data flow, and the models that make it think.

That curiosity pushed me toward Data Engineering and Machine Learning — designing pipelines that move data reliably at scale, and layering AI on top to build systems that don't just report on the world, but learn from it. I'm especially drawn to workflow automation and AI agents — tools that eliminate repetitive work and free people up to focus on problems that actually need a human brain.

I approach every problem with curiosity, attention to detail, and a bias toward building things that actually work. I'm not just chasing the next tool — I want to understand how things fit together end to end, from raw ingestion all the way to intelligent output.

Outside of data, I'm a proud fur daddy to 3 dogs and 1 cat. You'll find me outdoors fishing or hiking, spending time with family, picking up one new fact every weekend, and always keeping tabs on what's next in tech.
